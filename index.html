Material Science

Our Fabrics

Every great garment begins with the right material. We work with the finest cotton weaves, blends, and constructions engineered for durability, comfort, and character.

100%
Cotton
Pure Cotton
100% ring-spun cotton
Weight range: 4 – 14 oz
The foundation of our entire range. Pure ring-spun cotton delivers unmatched breathability, natural softness, and a handle that only improves with age. Sourced from trusted Indian mills, our cotton is the backbone of every denim and woven garment we produce — chosen for its consistency in weight, feel, and dyeability.

Whether it's a structured heavyweight selvedge or an airy lightweight chambray, 100% cotton offers the most natural hand-feel, excellent dye absorption for rich indigo shades, and long-term durability that synthetic blends cannot match.
Cotton
Lycra
Blend
🔄 Cotton + Lycra
98% cotton
2% elastane (Lycra)
Four-way stretch recovery
Four-way stretch meets natural cotton comfort. The addition of Lycra (spandex/elastane) gives this fabric exceptional recovery — it moves with the body and always returns to its original shape. Preferred for slim-fit, skinny, and body-contouring silhouettes where shape retention is critical.

Softer than pure denim with a silkier hand-feel, this blend holds colour exceptionally well through repeated washing cycles. The standard composition is approximately 98% cotton and 2% elastane, offering just enough stretch without losing the crisp, structured character of denim.
Cotton
Poly
Lycra
🔬 Tri-Blend
60–75% cotton
23–38% polyester
2% elastane (Lycra)
Performance + stretch
Engineered for performance and aesthetics in equal measure. Polyester enhances colour retention, moisture management, and dimensional stability — while Lycra ensures four-way stretch and recovery. This tri-blend is ideal for activewear-inspired fashion garments that demand both technical performance and a refined look.

Typically composed of 60–75% cotton, 23–38% polyester, and 2% elastane. Excellent for overdye and dark-wash finishes — the polyester component locks in colour depth while the cotton gives a natural, breathable base.

Weave Structures

3/1
Classic Twill

Three warp threads over one weft. The traditional denim construction — creates a strong diagonal rib with deep indigo face. Foundation of premium jeans.

2/1
Light Twill

Two over one — lighter, softer, with a subtle diagonal texture. Ideal for shirts and lightweight trousers requiring superior drape.

2/2
Balanced Twill

Equal interlacing produces a balanced, reversible diagonal rib. Excellent stretch recovery — preferred for tailored and fashion-forward cuts.

3/2
Hybrid Twill

Combines durability of 3/1 with the refinement of 2/2. Versatile across sportswear, fashion, and utility garments.

1×1
Plain Weave

The most fundamental structure. Creates a flat, smooth, highly breathable fabric. Used in chambray and lightweight woven shirts.

RHT
Right Hand Twill

Diagonal runs bottom-left to top-right — the standard for most denim. Firmer construction with a classic, structured hand-feel.

LHT
Left Hand Twill

Softer, more flexible, favoured in Japanese selvage-style denim for unique fading characteristics and superior comfort.

BKT
Broken Twill

Alternating twill directions create a zigzag pattern minimising fabric bias. Stable, warp-resistant — used in stretch denim to control skewing.

SAT
Satin Weave

Long float threads create a smooth, lustrous surface. Produces a silky, premium aesthetic on denim-cotton blends.

HBN
Herringbone

V-shaped zigzag twill creates a distinctive chevron pattern. Classic tailoring fabric adopted in premium denim for textural interest.

Fabric Weight Categories

We work across all weight categories — from airy summer fabrics to heavyweight selvedge-weight denim — each engineered for its intended end use.

4–9 oz
Lightweight Fabric

Airy, breathable, and perfect for warm-weather silhouettes. Drapes naturally, softens with every wash. Ideal for summer shirts and lightweight overshirts.

9–11.5 oz
Mid-weight Fabric

The workhorse of our range — structured enough for clean silhouettes, supple enough for all-day comfort. Suited to jeans, chinos, and casual shirts alike.

12+ oz
Heavyweight Fabric

Dense, substantial, and engineered for longevity. The backbone of premium raw denim and structured garments that develop a unique patina over time.

Technical Expertise in Various Washing Processes

Our Washing

From vintage stone-wash to precision laser finishing — our in-house washing unit delivers over a dozen advanced denim treatment processes with consistent, repeatable results at scale.

Each wash is precisely calibrated — controlled for temperature, duration, and chemistry — to achieve flawless, repeatable results across bulk production runs. Our in-house wash plant handles everything from a single prototype to tens of thousands of units.

Vintage Finishes
Stone Wash

Timeless, worn-in, authentic. Uses pumice stones to soften fabric and create a naturally faded, vintage look with distinct tactile character. One of the most requested finishes for casual and heritage-inspired denim collections.

Acid Wash

Bold, retro, distinctive. Creates high-contrast marbled patterns through oxidising agents — a standout vintage aesthetic that defined an era and returns stronger every season with fashion-forward collections.

Clean Finishes
Enzyme Wash

Clean, soft, refined. Bio-enzymes gently strip surface indigo dye, resulting in a smooth, polished finish with an eco-conscious footprint. Ideal for contemporary mid-weight denim where a subtle, lived-in softness is desired.

Rinse Wash

Minimal, sharp, classic. Removes excess surface dye while preserving deep, rich indigo tones — the cleanest finish for a dark, sophisticated look. The preferred choice for luxury denim that speaks through restraint and depth of colour.

Fashion Finishes
Distressed / Ripped

Edgy, raw, contemporary. Controlled abrasion, tearing and fraying applied with precision — creating an intentionally rugged, worn-out aesthetic for streetwear and statement denim collections.

Tint Wash

Subtle, modern, expressive. A soft colour overlay applied during washing creates unique tonal variation — ideal for seasonal colourways and fashion-forward collections seeking colour depth beyond raw indigo.

Ombré Wash

Gradient, fluid, artistic. Creates a seamless transition between shades across the garment — from deep indigo to pale sky. One of our most visually striking finishes, highly sought after in premium fashion collections.

Scraping / Sandblasting

Textured, artisanal, distinct. Surface dye is selectively removed through mechanical abrasion — creating handcrafted highlights and a genuinely artisanal surface quality that cannot be replicated digitally.

Advanced Finishes
Foil Printing

Metallic, bold, premium. Reflective foil applied with precision for a high-shine statement finish — elevating garments into collectible, fashion-forward pieces. Available in gold, silver, holographic, and custom colourways.

Spray Technique

Dynamic, controlled, modern. Localised fading and shading effects created by precise spray application — delivering dimensional contrast and artistic surface character with high repeatability across bulk production.

Laser Finishing

Precise, clean, innovative. Computer-controlled laser technology burns intricate patterns, fades, and details into denim with pixel-level precision and zero chemical waste — the most technically advanced finish we offer.

Who We Are

Rooted in
Rajasthan.
Reaching the world.

Deshnok Innovator was born from a simple but powerful idea — that a garment manufacturing unit in the heart of India could set global benchmarks. Founded in 2014, we began with a focused team, a sharp eye for quality, and an unwavering commitment to the craft of denim making.

Today, we are a fully integrated manufacturing partner trusted by leading Indian and international fashion brands. Our factory handles everything in-house — from sampling and cutting to stitching, dry processes, washing, finishing, and export-grade packing.

What sets us apart is not just our machinery or capacity — it is the people behind every seam. Our 500+ skilled artisans bring decades of collective experience to each garment, ensuring the kind of detail and precision that machines alone cannot deliver.
